{# Part-to-whole at a glance: what a period's money went on. A ring rather than bars because the question is "how does this divide up", and the answer wanted is the shape rather than a ranking. Six segments at most — past that adjacent slices blur — with the tail folded into «Այլ» rather than given a seventh colour nobody could tell from the sixth. The legend is not decoration and is not optional: it carries the name, the figure and the share for every segment, so identity is never colour alone. It is also the table view — somebody who cannot separate two of the hues reads the numbers. Every segment is a link to the payments behind it, in the ring and in the legend both: the first question about «Աշխատավարձ · 184,000» is which wages, and a chart that cannot be asked leaves the owner scrolling a list of everything to find out. Clicking the slice that is already showing goes back to the whole. It swaps the list rather than reloading the page. Answering "which wages" used to cost a fresh statistics page — thirteen queries, half a megabyte of HTML — and left the owner at the top of it, scrolling back down to the list they had just filtered. The `href` is still there and still correct: it is what the address bar is given, so a refresh keeps the filter and the back button undoes it, and it is what happens if the swap cannot run at all. Takes `donut` (from charts.donut) and an optional `donut_title`. #}
